Evolution Sandbox

Design custom genomes and simulate generational changes.

Method: quantitative genes mutate and recombine, then selection keeps higher-fitness genomes under the current environment. Fitness is based on climate match, resource demand, pollination traits, and morphology costs.

Growth Lab

Physiological simulation for any loaded plant, based on light, water, climate, nutrients, respiration, and stress.

Method: growth uses a simplified net primary productivity model: photosynthesis from light, CO2, and chlorophyll, reduced by Liebig-style limiting resources, respiration, age stage, and stress.
