Gardener's Corner

Essential Care Tips

Expert-curated techniques for growing healthy, beautiful flowers every season. Discover practical advice for every stage of your garden’s journey.

💧
Watering Wisdom
Deep, infrequent watering beats shallow daily sprinkling. Water at the base — never overhead — to prevent fungal disease. Check soil moisture 5cm deep: if still moist, wait. Morning watering reduces evaporation by 30%.
☀️
Light & Placement
Most flowers need 6–8 hours of direct sun. Observe your space throughout the day before planting — light shifts with seasons. South-facing beds (in northern hemisphere) offer the most consistent exposure. Reflected wall heat can extend the season by weeks.
🌱
Soil & Nutrition
Loamy, well-draining soil enriched with compost is the foundation. Feed spring-summer flowers with balanced NPK in early season, then switch to a phosphorus-rich feed mid-season to promote blooms over foliage. Never fertilise drought-stressed plants.
✂️
Deadheading
Removing spent blooms directs the plant's energy into new flowers rather than seed production. Use clean, sharp secateurs at 45° just above a leaf node or lateral bud. Some flowers — roses, dahlias, geraniums — can be coaxed to bloom months longer with diligent deadheading.
🐝
Pollinator Planting
A biodiverse garden is a resilient one. Mix single-flowered varieties (accessible to bees) with doubles. Plant in drifts of 5+ of the same species — pollinators work efficiently in clusters. Lavender, borage, and phacelia are exceptional bee magnets.
❄️
Winter Preparation
Mulch perennials with 8–10cm of straw or bark before first frost. Lift tender bulbs — dahlias, cannas, begonias — store cool and dry. Leave hollow stems standing for overwintering insects. Spring is for cutting back, not autumn — dead stems protect crowns from frost.
